💡 How MemoryBridge Analyzes Deviations Without Diagnosis

Traditional health tech attempts complex biometric and psychological diagnoses which risk false alarms and anxiety. MemoryBridge operates strictly on spatial sequence invariants: detecting repetitive oscillating transits (such as wandering between rooms repeatedly) or omission of primary morning anchor locations (such as the kitchen). The alerts inform families or caregivers naturally and calmly.
